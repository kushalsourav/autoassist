## diagnose_symptoms
A complaint-oriented assistant skill. Discuss possible causes as hypotheses, ask targeted follow-ups such as fault codes or circumstances, and recommend appropriate professional diagnosis. The classifier provides weak complaint-component labels, not diagnostic probabilities. If brakes, steering or loss of power affect safety, give immediate safe-stop guidance before any data collection. Do not insist on year/make/model unless a model-specific recall search is actually needed. Tools: `classify_symptoms`, optionally `search_recalls` after vehicle identity is complete.
